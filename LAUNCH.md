**Release notes**

EasyMidEvil is a free building generator for Blender 5.3. Pick a cottage, tavern, blacksmith, round tower, or castle keep. Resize it in the viewport, change its variation, or drop in all five with Create Starter Village. This version adds live editing, updated wood and roof mapping, and blue-grey windows. For a building made in 0.1, use Create Editable Copy to leave the original intact.

Download `EasyMidEvil-0.2.0.zip` from Assets and install it from disk in Blender. Exterior models only.
